# Inter

`Inter-500.ttf` and `Inter-700.ttf` are static Latin subsets of Inter by
Rasmus Andersson (https://rsms.me/inter/), used for the site's social card
(`app/opengraph-image.tsx`). Inter is licensed under the SIL Open Font
